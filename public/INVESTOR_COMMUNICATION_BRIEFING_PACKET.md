## 4. INVESTOR DUE DILIGENCE FAQ & DEFENSIVE TALKING POINTS

Be prepared to answer these common diligence inquiries with precise, disciplined language:

#### Q1: "Is a sample of 20 golfers too small to validate a commercial business?"
> **Response:**  
> *"Our 20-golfer discovery study was designed strictly as qualitative problem discovery and workflow validation, following the Lean Startup and Mom Test methodologies. We reached thematic saturation by the 14th interview, where no new failure modes were emerging. We do not claim population census statistics; rather, we established that 80% had actively purchased commercial aids and 87.5% abandoned them due to setup friction and discomfort. That insight drove our exact product specifications. Commercial demand will be quantitatively proven through our pre-order funnel and 1,420-unit break-even target."*

#### Q2: "Why did your interview protocol test a 10–22 handicap when your target market is 10–20?"
> **Response:**  
> *"Our commercial core target is the 10–20 handicap index—dedicated mid-level golfers with regular practice habits. In our research protocol, we deliberately included boundary testing up to 22 (specifically evaluating participant M18 at a 21 handicap) to test whether setup friction and price sensitivity diverged at the upper boundary. The data proved that players at 21 HCP experienced the exact same early casting and launch monitor confusion as players at 11 HCP, confirming broad product utility across the entire mid-handicap range."*

#### Q3: "What prevents golfers from using cheaper makeshift hacks like alignment rods or towels?"
> **Response:**  
> *"Golfers already use towels and alignment sticks—in fact, M02 and M08 explicitly improvised with them. However, towel drills only address body connection; they cannot provide progressive resistance against wrist casting or dynamic loft inflation. Golfers invent DIY hacks precisely because no commercial aid provides tactile resistance without multi-strap complexity. Golf Resistance turns that makeshift workaround into a durable, engineered product."*

#### Q4: "Are you promising that your training aid will lower a golfer's handicap?"
> **Response:**  
> *"No, and we are disciplined about not making unverified medical or performance claims. We promise a convenient, habit-forming practice workflow that allows a golfer to execute 30 high-quality, coach-aligned motor reps in under 10 minutes at home. By eliminating setup friction and providing progressive tactile resistance, we improve practice consistency between lessons. On-course scoring improvements will be evaluated during our 60-day PGA facility pilot."*
